Privacy Policy

Form Submission Validator only processes the form fields and required field list submitted in the request in order to return a structured validation result.

  • The app does not store submitted form data.
  • The app does not use submitted form data for training.
  • The app does not sell user data.
  • The app does not call external APIs.
  • The app does not access external systems.
  • The app does not submit forms.
  • The app does not fill missing fields.
  • The app is read-only.
  • The app does not require login or user accounts.
