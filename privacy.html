Privacy Policy for Form Submission Validator

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data Collected

Form Submission Validator processes only the form data submitted by the user in the current request.

The expected tool inputs are:

  • fields: an object containing submitted form field names and values.
  • required_fields: an array of field names that must be present and non-empty for the form to be considered ready to submit.

The app does not collect data from external sources, does not scrape websites, and does not fetch additional data.

2. Tool Outputs

The app may return the following structured fields:

  • can_submit: whether the submitted fields satisfy the required-field validation rules.
  • missing_fields: required fields that are missing, empty, or null.
  • invalid_fields: fields that fail validation checks, if applicable.
  • errors: structured validation or scope errors, if applicable.

These outputs are generated only from the user-provided input.

3. Purpose of Processing

The submitted data is used only to validate whether the provided form fields satisfy the required submission conditions.

The app does not use submitted data for unrelated purposes, marketing, profiling, advertising, or training-specific user workflows.

The app does not decide whether a user should submit a form. It only checks the provided payload against explicit required-field rules.

4. Recipients and Sharing

The app does not sell user data.

The app does not intentionally share submitted data with third-party recipients.

The app does not call external APIs.

The app does not send submitted fields, validation results, or extracted values to downstream systems.

The app does not contact customers, applicants, form owners, service providers, or other external parties.

The app does not update forms, records, databases, calendars, email systems, ticketing systems, CRMs, or other external systems.

5. Retention

The app is designed to be stateless.

The app does not store submitted form data.

The app does not store submitted inputs or generated outputs after request processing is complete, aside from transient platform-level request handling and logs.

The app does not create user profiles or maintain historical records of submitted forms.

6. User Controls

Users control what they submit to the app.

Users should avoid submitting sensitive or unnecessary personal data.

Users may remove or redact sensitive details before submitting form fields for validation.

Users may choose not to use the app if they do not want their submitted fields processed for validation.

Privacy questions or deletion requests can be sent to: sidcraigau@gmail.com

7. Login and Accounts

The app does not require user accounts, authentication, login credentials, or account creation.

8. Read-Only Operation and No Side Effects

The app is read-only.

No external APIs are called.

No downstream writes are performed.

The app does not submit forms.

The app does not fill missing fields.

The app does not rewrite user input.

The app does not modify records, send messages, make decisions, contact external parties, or perform operational actions.

9. Contact
