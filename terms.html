Terms of Use

This app is provided for structured validation of form submission readiness.

  • Users are responsible for reviewing validation outputs before using them in real submissions.
  • The app does not guarantee that every possible form rule or business requirement will be detected.
  • The app does not provide legal, financial, medical, or professional advice.
  • The app should not be used as the only validation layer for critical submissions.
  • The app does not submit data to external systems.
  • The app does not fill missing fields or rewrite user input.
  • The app is read-only and has no write actions.
